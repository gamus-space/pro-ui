About

The combined entry contains content from games:

  • Diablo
  • Diablo: Hellfire

This is an isometric 2D action RPG game. The hero (either warrior, rogue or sorcerer) faces a quest to destroy Diablo, the Lord of Terror.

The goal of the expansion is to first eliminate Na-Krul that was accidentally released by a sorcerer. New character classes will help in this additional quest.

Music

The game has the soundtrack in packaged 22kHz 16bit stereo WAV files composed by:

  • Matt Uelmen
  • Craig Utterback

For the expansion tracks, it is not clear who the author is. The original composer appears is WAV files metadata, but he's not credited in the game.

Graphics

The game uses resolution of 640x480 pixels with 256 colors.

Version specifics

The PC version was the base release.
