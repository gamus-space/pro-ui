About

This is a 3D action RPG game.

In the year 1000, crusaders are unexpectedly attacked by monsters. MacBride, a brave adventurer, sets out on a quest to investigate the incident. Soon, loyal companions join him to form a powerful party capable of defeating the evil sorcerer.

Music

The music is composed by:

The music is stored in RAW uncompressed format with the quality of 11kHz 16bit mono.

Graphics

The game uses resolution of 640x480 pixels with 256 colors.

Its 3D voxel-based rendering engine was a rare feature at the time, breaking away from the industry standard of traditional polygons.
