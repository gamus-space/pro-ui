About

This is a sequel to a third-person perspective 3D shooter/platform game.

Just after Kurt Hectic saved the earth from the first alien invasion, another minecrawler appears. Supposedly this is yet another job for the protagonist, however things don't really go as planned. Kurt will need the assistance of his fellow companions: cybernetic dog Max and doctor Hawkins.

Music

The game has a soundtrack composed by:

  • Jesper Kyd
  • Albert Olson
  • Raymond Watts

The music is stored in proprietary ADPCM-compressed MUS files with quality of 22kHz 16-bit stereo.

Graphics

Game uses OpenGL rendering engine. The featured version uses Voodoo 3 rendering with resolution of 640x480 with 64k colors.
