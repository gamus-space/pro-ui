About

This is the third game in Lemmings series.

The game tells the story of the Classic, Shadow, and Egyptian tribes flying on the Ark and being dropped onto their respective islands to find their new home.

Music

The CD-ROM release has CD Audio tracks composed by:

  • Raymond Usher

According to some sources on the Internet, Colin Anderson arranged the music for PC sound cards.

The original floppy release has OPL3 music available at gamus.space.

Classic tribe tunes are new versions of Lemmings tracks, respectively:

  • One Way or Another
  • Take Good Care of My Lemmings
  • Postcard from Lemmingland

Graphics

The game uses 320x200 resolution with 256 colors. Menus use resolution of 320x240. Map screen uses 640x480 in SVGA mode.

Version specifics

The game had floppy release with MIDI music.

The featured CD-ROM release also plays MIDI music during cinematics.
