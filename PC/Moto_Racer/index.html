About

An arcade motorcycle racer featuring eight bikes competing across eight tracks.

A free DLC expansion pack later introduced two additional circuits.

Game uses Direct3D rendering engine. The featured version uses Voodoo 3 rendering with resolution of 640x480 with 64k colors.
