About

This is a continuation of a mainstream turn-based artillery game. Actually it's the last 2D part of the series.

Fourth part introduces new terrain types, weapons and game modes.

Music

Game has audio tracks composed by:

  • Bjørn Lynne

Music is stored in uncompressed WAV files with quality of 16bit stereo and sampling rate varying from 16kHz to 28kHz.

The tracks have exactly the same structure as in Worms Armageddon. Some tracks have been reused and some have their new versions.

Graphics

The game allows to pick resolution of 640x480, 800x600 (featured) or 1024x768 pixels with 256 colors. Menus use resolution of 640x480.

Featured single player modes are Training, Time Attack and Missions.
